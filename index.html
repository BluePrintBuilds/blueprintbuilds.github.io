The core, done right

Three things it does — and nothing it shouldn't.

No marketplace. No CRM. No estimating. No generic AI. Just the underserved fundamentals of construction delivery, built to hold up under scrutiny.

Zero-signal field capture

Photos, voice notes and site diary entries save to the device instantly — offline-first. When a connection returns, everything syncs in order. Basements and remote sites are no longer blind spots.

Decisions on the record

Every piece of evidence is time-stamped, attributed to its submitter and kept on an append-only audit trail. Variations reach the client with their cost and time impact, and every answer is sequenced and recorded — a defensible record, not a folder of loose photos.

Role-scoped client views

Owners, project managers, site leads and clients each see a view scoped to their role. Clients get progress, drawings and decisions — clarity and confidence without the noise, and without ever touching data they shouldn't.

The privacy pledge

Privacy-first by default. Cookieless by design.

The Blueprint Builds app runs zero ad SDKs and zero third-party trackers. This website loads no third-party scripts and sets no cookies — none. Your project data lives in private, access-controlled storage and travels only over encrypted connections.
