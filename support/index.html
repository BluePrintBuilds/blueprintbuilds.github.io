Help

Blueprint Builds support

Last updated 10 September 2026

Blueprint Builds is Peninsula Equine's field app for staff and clients. If you need help signing in, want to report a problem, or want your account or data removed, we're easy to reach.

Email: info@peninsulaequine.systems

Signing in

Access is invitation-only: an administrator adds your work email to the staff or client list before your account is created. If your sign-in fails, check you are using the invited email address, then contact us and we'll reset or re-issue access.

Notifications

The app asks permission to send push notifications about project decisions that involve you. Declining leaves the app fully functional — you can change this at any time in your device's notification settings.

Delete your account

In the signed-in app, open Account / More → Support & legal → Request account deletion. Confirming there securely records a permanent deletion request without requiring an email or phone call. Requests are completed within 30 days and completion is confirmed to the registered email. Project evidence that must remain part of the construction record may be retained independently of the active account, as described in the Privacy Policy. If you cannot sign in, contact support so we can restore access to the in-app process.

Reporting a problem

Email a short description of what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps. We aim to reply within one business day.
