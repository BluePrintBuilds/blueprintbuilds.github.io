Blueprint Builds support

Last updated 18 September 2026

Blueprint Builds is an invitation-only construction delivery app for authorised project teams and clients. If you need help signing in, want to report a problem, or want your account or data removed, Blueprint Builds Support is easy to reach.

Email: support@blueprintbuilds.app

Signing in

Access is invitation-only: an administrator adds your work email to the staff or client list before your account is created. If your sign-in fails, check you are using the invited email address, then contact us and we'll reset or re-issue access.

Understanding the project record

Blueprint keeps the current drawing, site evidence, client decisions and recorded milestones with their project. Your existing CAD or engineering software remains the place to create the design.

Plans and site evidence

Use the issued drawing and check its revision before marking up a detail. Capture a photo, voice note or site diary against the authorised project. Prepare offline plans before leaving signal, and check the device save status before closing unsaved work.

Progress and client decisions

Progress follows the milestones entered by the build team. Decisions state the proposed change and its cost or time impact. The client sees the information and decisions intended for them; staff tools and internal workspace controls remain restricted.

Handover and Build Story

Photos, milestones and decisions provide the source records for handover. A Build Story stays private until sharing is deliberately enabled. A polished story does not replace the underlying project evidence, and a workspace link does not grant access by itself.

Notifications

The app asks permission to send push notifications about project decisions that involve you. Declining leaves the app fully functional — you can change this at any time in your device's notification settings.

Delete your account

In the signed-in app, open Account / More → Support & legal → Request account deletion. Confirming there securely records a permanent deletion request without requiring an email or phone call. Requests are completed within 30 days and completion is confirmed to the registered email. Project evidence that must remain part of the construction record may be retained independently of the active account, as described in the Privacy Policy. If you cannot sign in, contact support so we can restore access to the in-app process.

Reporting a problem

Email a short description of what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps us investigate the issue and reply through the support inbox.
