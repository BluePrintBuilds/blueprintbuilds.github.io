Terms of Use

Blueprint Builds mobile app · Last updated 2 September 2026

Who may use the app

Blueprint Builds is provided to invited Peninsula Equine staff and clients for recording and reviewing project evidence and status. There is no public sign-up. Accounts are issued and revoked by Peninsula Equine at its discretion.

Project records

Evidence entries — including notes, photos, voice recordings and decision responses — are project records. Once submitted they may not be edited or deleted by the person who created them, so that the project's audit trail stays accurate and complete.

Acceptable use

Use the app only for legitimate project work in the workspace you have been invited to. Do not attempt to access projects, records or roles beyond those assigned to your account.

Questions and access changes

Contact ciro@procasa.com.au with questions about this app or to request access changes. See the Privacy Policy for how account data is handled and how to request deletion.
