Privacy Policy

Blueprint Builds mobile app · Last updated 2 September 2026

Blueprint Builds is a private, invitation-only field app used by Peninsula Equine staff and their construction clients. It is not available for public sign-up. This policy describes what the app collects and how that information is used.

Information we collect

  • Work email address and password, used only to sign you in (processed by our database provider, Supabase, acting on our behalf). Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's secure keychain.
  • Project evidence notes you type while using the app, linked to your account and the relevant project.
  • Photos and voice recordings you choose to attach to an evidence note. These are stored in private, access-controlled storage, linked to your account and project, and are only viewable by authorised members of your workspace. The camera, microphone and photo library are accessed only when you actively choose to capture or attach something.
  • A device push-notification token, stored only if you allow notifications, used solely to tell you about project decisions that involve you. You can withdraw this at any time in your device's notification settings.
  • Standard security and technical logs (e.g. request time, route, error status) generated by our hosting and database providers. These do not include your note content or password.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, or payment information through the app, and we do not access your camera, microphone or photos in the background.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tracking, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

How information is used

  • To authenticate you and show you the correct projects and role-based views.
  • To keep an accurate, auditable record of site evidence for the project it was recorded against.

Retention and deletion

  • Evidence records, including attached photos and voice recordings, form part of the project's construction record and are retained for the life of the project and Peninsula Equine's standard record-keeping period.
  • To close your account or request deletion of your account data, contact info@peninsulaequine.systems or ciro@procasa.com.au. Access is also removed automatically when an administrator removes you from the staff allow list. See also Support — delete your account.

This website

This website is a static informational site. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers, analytics or external fonts.
